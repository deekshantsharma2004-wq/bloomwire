Refunds Policy

Returns, replacements, and cancellations.

Refunds and returns are accepted only for damaged, wrong, or defective items reported within 48 hours of delivery. Since each BloomWire piece is handcrafted, made-to-order or boutique-run items are not eligible for returns for a change of mind.

To request a refund, email hello@bloomwire.in with your order number, a short description of the issue, and clear photos. If the issue is authenticated, we will arrange a refund to your original payment method or a replacement item where available.

For prepaid orders, refunds are usually processed within 5–7 business days after the returned parcel is inspected. Orders cancelled before dispatch can be refunded in full; once dispatched, the order will be treated under the standard returns process.
