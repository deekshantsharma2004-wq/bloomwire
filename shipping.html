Shipping Policy

Dispatch, delivery, and care standards.

BloomWire dispatches handcrafted floral keepsakes within 24–48 hours from Jaipur, India. Orders above ₹499 qualify for free shipping across India. Orders below this threshold are charged a nominal courier fee.

We partner with trusted domestic courier services and share tracking information once the order is packed and dispatched. Delivery times vary by location and are typically 2–6 business days for metro cities and 4–8 business days for non-metro locations.

If your parcel arrives damaged, please report it within 48 hours with unboxing photos and your order number. We will arrange a replacement or refund, subject to inspection.
