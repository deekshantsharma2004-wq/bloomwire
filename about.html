Our story

Jaipur-made memories in everlasting wire.

BloomWire began with a simple idea: create flowers that can last as long as the feeling they represent. Inspired by Jaipur craftsmanship, every bloom is hand-woven with care.

We combine premium chenille wire, elegant color palettes, and a romantic artisan finish to make each piece feel personal, gentle, and enduring.

Today, BloomWire is a premium keepsake studio for birthdays, anniversaries, thank-yous, and one-of-a-kind gifting moments.
